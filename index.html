Why choose us

Three pillars behind every program we run

Education - Clinical - Technology

Education - Education is our first prescription.

We go through your results with you, one line at a time — what each number means, why yours looks the way it does, and what actually moves it. You leave with a written plan in plain language that’s yours to keep, and a real understanding of how your body works.

Clinical - Nothing begins without the full picture.

Every program starts with complete bloodwork, an InBody 970 body composition scan, and a full evaluation with your provider before anything is prescribed — even IV therapy and vitamin injections. Your plan follows a written protocol built from published medical evidence. Follow-up visits and repeat labs are scheduled from day one.

Technology - Your health, all in one place.

Your health journey deserves clarity, convenience, and a personal connection. Securely access your lab results, clinical notes, body scans, and progress anytime, all in one place. Track your improvements, celebrate milestones, and stay connected with your care team through convenient messaging for guidance and support at every step of your journey.

About Maikel

Maikel began his medical career in Cuba, where he earned his medical degree in 2004 and practiced as a physician for three years, with postgraduate training in Intensive Care Medicine. His early clinical experience provided a strong foundation in medical diagnosis, human physiology, and the management of critically ill patients.

After continuing his professional journey in the United States, he earned his Master of Science in Nursing from Miami Regional University and became a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner through the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board (AANPCB). He has since practiced as a nurse practitioner in Florida for five years.

His experience across both ends of the clinical spectrum—from critical care to primary care—has given him a unique perspective on the profound impact of chronic diseases, particularly cardiovascular and metabolic conditions, and the importance of prevention and early intervention.

These experiences have shaped his clinical philosophy and commitment to a more proactive, patient-centered approach to healthcare. Today, at Global Reach Clinic, he focuses on comprehensive, preventive, personalized, and concierge medicine, emphasizing individualized care, long-term wellness, and the identification of modifiable health risks.

At the heart of his practice is a strong belief that patient education is one of the most powerful tools in preventive medicine. He believes that well-informed patients are better equipped to make sound healthcare decisions, adopt sustainable lifestyle changes, and take an active role in protecting their long-term health.
